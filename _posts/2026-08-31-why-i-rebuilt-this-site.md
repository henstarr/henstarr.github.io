---
title: Why I Rebuilt This Site
---

For a while this domain was a portfolio page: a headshot, a project grid, a résumé link. Useful, but static in the worst sense — it only ever changed when I updated a job title.

I'd rather have a place to actually write: short notes on what I'm building, things I run into at work, projects that don't fit neatly on a résumé bullet. So this is that place now. The old bio and work history didn't disappear — they moved to [About](/about/) — but the front page is posts, in order, newest first.

If you want new posts by email rather than checking back, there's a subscribe box at the bottom of every page. No account, no tracking beyond the address itself — just an email when something new goes up.
